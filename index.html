Profile

About

I am an Agricultural Engineer with earlier education in Mechatronics and Electronics Engineering. My transition into agricultural research began in 2021 when I joined the Precision Agriculture & Analytics Lab (PAAL) at the University of Agriculture Faisalabad.

I initially worked with research infrastructure and field sensing, including equipment requirements, weather monitoring, UAV platforms, and multispectral and thermal sensors. This gradually led me into satellite remote sensing, geospatial data processing, and process-based crop modelling, where I became particularly interested in connecting observations of crop state and water use with mechanistic simulations.

I completed an MSc (Hons) in Agricultural Engineering in 2025. My research focused on assimilating satellite-derived LAI and actual evapotranspiration into CropSyst for wheat biomass and yield estimation. Alongside this work, I have contributed to applied crop monitoring, UAV field activities, climate and hazard analyses, and agriculture decision support systems.
